# PayPal Seed — System & Implementation Plan

## 1. Executive Summary
PayPal Seed enables mutual aid, community savings streaks, fair underwriting, micro-lending, hardship relief, and community safety reserves backed by transparent ledgering and PayPal payment rails.

## 2. Core Pillars
1. **Savings Streaks (F1)**: Micro-saving targets, streaks, and pause grace periods.
2. **Lending Ladder (F2, F4)**: Graduated credit sizing, fair scheduling, and automated repayment.
3. **Consensual Underwriting (F3)**: Fair assessments, income smoothing, no predatory scoring.
4. **Hardship Rescheduling (F5)**: Proactive guardian signals, flexible reschedule engine.
5. **Portable Credit Record (F6)**: Cryptographically verified community standing certificates.
6. **Double-Entry Ledger (F7a)**: Immutable posting rules, strict balance guarantees.
7. **Two-Tier Governance (F7)**: Maker-checker verification, agent proposal vetting.
8. **Community Safety Reserve (F8)**: Donated safety funds, transparent coverage mechanisms.
9. **Fraud & Abuse Detection (F10)**: Real-time signals via Elastic, automated dispute bundles.
